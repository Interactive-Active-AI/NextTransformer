# Code of conduct

NextTransformer adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in this project's repository, discussions and community channels is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and disagree about ideas rather than
people. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers: send a direct message to a maintainer in a community channel
(see the [community page](https://interactive-active-ai.github.io/NextTransformer/docs/community/)), or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
